## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Our proposed solution is FarmConnect, a digital platform that connects farmers directly with consumers, retailers, supermarkets and bulk buyers.
- Farmers can list their agricultural products with quantity, quality and price.
- Consumers can purchase fresh products directly from farmers.
- Bulk buyers can place large orders through the platform.
- The platform provides logistics support for product delivery.
- AI helps farmers predict product demand and reduce wastage.
Innovation and Uniqueness:

FarmConnect reduces the need for multiple intermediaries, allowing farmers to earn better profits while consumers receive products at affordable prices.

## Technical Approach
We will develop a user-friendly web and mobile application.

Technologies to be used:
- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- Backend: Python with Django
- Database: MySQL
- AI/ML: Python for demand forecasting
- Maps API: For route optimization
- Cloud Hosting: For storing and managing data
Implementation Process:
1. Farmers register and upload their products.
2. Buyers browse available products and compare prices.
3. Buyers place orders directly with farmers.
4. The system connects orders with logistics providers.
5. AI predicts demand and suggests efficient delivery routes
## Feasibility and Viability
Feasibility:

The solution can be developed using existing web technologies, mobile applications and AI tools. It can be implemented in stages, starting with a small group of farmers.

Potential Challenges:
- Lack of digital knowledge among some farmers.
- Transportation and delivery difficulties.
- Maintaining product quality.
- Internet connectivity in rural areas.
Strategies to Overcome Challenges:
- Provide a simple, multilingual interface.
- Partner with local transport services.
- Include product quality verification.
- Support low-internet usage and mobile access.

## Impact and Benefits
Economic Benefits:
- Farmers receive better prices for their products.
- Consumers can purchase products at lower prices.
- Reduces unnecessary intermediary costs.
Social Benefits:
- Supports farmers and rural communities.
- Encourages digital literacy.
- Creates opportunities for local businesses.
Environmental Benefits:
- Reduces food wastage.
- Optimizes transportation routes.
- Supports efficient use of resources.
